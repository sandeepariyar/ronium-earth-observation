/ 01 — The Brief

A changing planet.
A clearer
perspective.

There is no shortage of information about our planet. The challenge is finding context: where to look, what changed, and what a signal can actually tell us. RONIUM makes that exploration tangible.

From distant signals. To visible patterns. To human understanding.

◆ Objective

Make complexity legible. Explore the relationship between orbital systems, imagery and decisions through a carefully designed spatial interface.

◆ Approach

Build the experience. Combine interactive graphics with a working public-imagery console. Keep simulated states and real data sources clearly distinguished.
